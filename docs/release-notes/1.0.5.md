## Improved

- Faster cold launches: last-known scores appear immediately on open from a local snapshot while fresh updates load in the background.
- Smoother, sharper team logos with in-memory and disk caching (downsampled to display resolution instead of re-fetching on every scroll or day switch).
- Lower CPU, memory, and battery usage: polling and timers pause completely when the menu-bar window is hidden or your Mac is offline, and refresh immediately on reopen or reconnect.
- Faster live updates on Today: future days (+1 to +3) load on demand in separate lanes so slow schedule requests never delay live games, and unchanged CDN responses reuse cached data via HTTP `304 Not Modified`.
- Smarter box-score loading: expanded and live games fetch player leaders first, while settled final games skip redundant re-fetches.
